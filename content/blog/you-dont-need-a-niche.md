Most people who want to start on social media don't have a content problem.

They have a **waiting** problem.

Waiting to pick a niche. Waiting to feel ready. Waiting until the bio sounds smart enough that strangers will take them seriously.

I built Your Blueprint because that wait is where most people die. Not because they had nothing to say — because they never picked **one** thing to say first.

## What to do instead

Forget the niche for a week.

Pick one story that actually happened to you. Something small and true. You saved money a certain way. You learned a tool the hard way. You helped a friend with something you thought was obvious.

Write it like you'd tell that friend. Not like a brand.

Then post it.

That's the whole first move. Not a content calendar. Not a 12-month strategy. One story, in public, so you can see who talks back.

## Why this works

A niche is a conclusion. You get it **after** you talk for a while, not before.

Your first posts are how you find out what you sound like when you're not hiding. The Blueprint is just a faster way to name that — 18 questions about you, then a starting point that isn't generic.
